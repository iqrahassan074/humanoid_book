# Humanoid Robots: Bringing AI Into the Physical World

Humanoid robots combine:
- Mechanical engineering  
- Sensors  
- Motors  
- AI brains  

## Core Components

### 1. Sensors  
Robots “see” the world using:
- Cameras  
- LIDAR  
- Touch sensors  
- Microphones  

### 2. Actuators  
Motors that mimic human movement:
- Walking  
- Running  
- Gestures  
- Lifting objects  

### 3. Perception System
AI interprets the world:
- Object detection  
- Depth perception  
- Spatial understanding  

### 4. Behavior System
Decides:
- How to respond  
- How to move  
- How to talk  

## Why Humanoid Robots Matter
- Elderly care  
- Household assistance  
- Dangerous jobs  
- Education  
- Companionship
