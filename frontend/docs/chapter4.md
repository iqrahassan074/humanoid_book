# AI Safety, Ethics & Human Trust

Human-like AI is powerful — and with power comes responsibility.

## 1. Privacy
AI must:
- Not expose personal info  
- Protect user data  
- Avoid unwanted data collection  

## 2. Safety Controls
Modern AI uses:
- Reinforcement training  
- Guardrails  
- Content moderation  
- Ethical rules  

## 3. Avoiding Harm
AI must avoid:
- Violence  
- Manipulation  
- Misinformation  
- Harassment  

## 4. Human-Centered Design
AI should uplift people, not replace them.

## 5. Transparency
Users need to know:
- How AI works  
- What limitations it has  
- Where its data came from
