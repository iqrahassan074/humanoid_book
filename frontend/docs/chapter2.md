# Neural Networks: The Digital Brain

Neural networks are inspired by the biology of the human brain.  
Just like neurons fire and pass signals, artificial neurons process data.

## How Neural Networks Work

### 1. **Input Layer**
Raw data enters (text, audio, images).

### 2. **Hidden Layers**
The network:
- Detects patterns  
- Learns relationships  
- Builds “representations”  

### 3. **Output Layer**
AI gives a prediction or answer.

## Types of Neural Networks

### 🔹 Feedforward Networks
Simple and fast — used for basic tasks.

### 🔹 Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
Good for images, object detection, medical scans.

### 🔹 Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)
Good for sequences like speech and text.

### 🔹 Transformers (Modern SOTA)
The model used in ChatGPT and Gemini:
- Understands long context  
- Learns relationships across sentences  
- Generates human-quality text  

Transformers are the **main reason** today’s AI feels human.
